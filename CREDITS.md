# Credits

## Sources cited in the guides

Every guide in `app/data/trophies.json` cites its sources by number; the guide pane links them. The facts were
paraphrased; no guide text was copied. The roadmap's hour estimates (story, 100% checklist, strands) are PowerPyx's,
and the Missables page's "what cannot be missed" points cite PowerPyx and OutputLag by name.

- PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap: https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/
- OutputLag — GTA 6 Trophy List (Steven Mills): https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-list/
- Official trophy list (PlayStation), as listed by Exophase: https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/
  (also the source of the "hidden on PSN" flags)
- PSNProfiles — Grand Theft Auto VI trophy list: https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi
- Beebom — GTA 6 Trophy List (Bipradeep Biswas): https://beebom.com/gta-6-trophy-achievements-list/
- Insider Gaming — Complete GTA 6 Trophy List (Charlie Champion):
  https://insider-gaming.com/complete-gta-6-trophy-list-how-to-get-all-trophies-achievements/
- CULTR — Complete GTA 6 Trophy List (Oliver Tryon):
  https://www.cultr.com/news/complete-gta-6-trophy-list-every-trophy-and-achievement/

All pages were read on 19 Nov 2026; the date is kept once per source in the data's `sources` registry and shown on
every source line. PSNProfiles, Beebom, Insider Gaming and CULTR only list the official text (names, grades and/or
descriptions), so the guide pane shows them as a cross-check of exactly that, never as a source for how-to facts.
The "Sources & save" page lists the same registry in one table (the official list and Exophase's hidden flags share
one page and one row); pages we could not read (Push Square, KeenGamer) are not listed as sources.

## Official text

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them. Everything else (kinds,
roadmap stages, missable calls, guides and steps) was written by SUNLINE.

## Code, fonts and images

No third-party code, libraries, fonts or images are used. The app uses system fonts only and ships no font file: its
stack asks for Ubuntu Sans first (narrower, so the official descriptions fit without being cut) and falls back to
system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial and the default sans-serif. The medal marks are inline SVG drawn
by the app. There is no Rockstar logo, wordmark, artwork, screenshot or lookalike font.
